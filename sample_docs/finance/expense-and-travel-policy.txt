Expense and Travel Policy
=========================

Department: Finance
Owner: Finance Operations
Version: 2026.1

This policy explains which business expenses Lumora Systems reimburses and how to claim them.
All expenses must be reasonable, necessary for business, and supported by an itemized receipt.

SUBMITTING EXPENSES

Submit expenses in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase date. Receipts are required for
every expense above $25. Expenses submitted more than 90 days after purchase will not be
reimbursed. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

APPROVAL LIMITS

Managers can approve individual expenses up to $2,500. Expenses between $2,500 and $10,000
require approval from the department head. Anything above $10,000 must be approved by the CFO
and requires a purchase order.

TRAVEL BOOKING

All flights and hotels must be booked through Navan, the corporate travel platform. Economy class
is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours
or longer. Business class requires VP approval.

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to $250 per night in standard cities and up to $350 per night in
high-cost cities such as New York, San Francisco, London and Zurich.

MEALS AND PER DIEM

While traveling, meals are reimbursed up to a daily limit of $75. Alcohol is not reimbursable
except at approved client entertainment events. Team meals require the most senior attendee to
pay and submit the expense.

NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES

Personal entertainment, traffic and parking fines, upgrades not approved in advance, and
expenses for family members traveling with the employee are not reimbursed.

CORPORATE CARDS

Employees who travel more than four times a year may request a corporate card. Corporate card
transactions must be reconciled in Expensify within 15 days of the statement date.
