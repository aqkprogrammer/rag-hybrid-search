# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

Lumora Systems is a remote-first company. Most roles can be performed fully remotely; some
roles are designated hybrid and require regular office attendance.

## Eligibility

All employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period are eligible to work remotely,
unless their role is designated as hybrid in their offer letter. Hybrid employees are expected
in a Lumora office at least 2 days per week, typically Tuesday and Thursday.

## Core Collaboration Hours

Regardless of location, employees must be available between 10:00 and 14:00 in their team's
primary time zone for meetings and synchronous collaboration.

## Working From Another Country

Employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up to 30 days in
any 12-month period. Longer stays require prior written approval from People Operations and
Legal because of tax and immigration implications. Working from sanctioned countries is never
permitted.

## Home Office Stipend

Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $1,000 (or local equivalent) to
purchase a desk, chair and monitor, plus a monthly internet allowance of $60. The stipend is
claimed through the expense system with receipts within 90 days of the start date.

## Security While Working Remotely

- Only use company-managed devices to access internal systems.
- Always connect through the corporate VPN when on public or shared Wi-Fi networks.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device.
- Do not let family members or other people use your work laptop.

## Coworking Spaces

Employees without access to a Lumora office may expense a coworking membership of up to $250
per month with manager approval.
