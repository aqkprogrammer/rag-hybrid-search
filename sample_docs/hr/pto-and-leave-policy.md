# Paid Time Off & Leave Policy

This policy describes how Lumora Systems employees accrue, request and use paid time off (PTO)
and other forms of leave. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees in the United
States, Canada and the United Kingdom. Contractors are covered by their agency agreements.

## Paid Time Off

### Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per month, for a total of 21 days per calendar year.
Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata based on their contracted hours. Accrual begins on the
first day of employment, and PTO can be used as soon as it has accrued.

After five years of continuous service, the accrual rate increases to 2.25 days per month
(27 days per year).

### Carry-over and payout

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days
must be used by March 31, after which they expire. Unused PTO above the carry-over limit is
forfeited on January 1 unless local law requires otherwise.

On termination, accrued but unused PTO is paid out in the final paycheck in jurisdictions where
payout is required by law, including California and the United Kingdom.

### Requesting time off

PTO requests must be submitted in Workday at least 10 business days in advance for absences
longer than three consecutive days. Shorter absences require at least 2 business days of notice.
Managers must approve or decline a request within 3 business days.

During the annual release freeze (the last two weeks of December), engineering teams should
coordinate PTO so that each on-call rotation keeps at least two available engineers.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, which do not
carry over. A doctor's note is only required for absences longer than 5 consecutive working
days. Sick days may also be used to care for a sick family member.

## Parental Leave

Lumora offers 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave to birthing parents and 16 weeks of fully
paid leave to non-birthing parents, including adoptive and foster parents. Parental leave must
be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement and can be split into at most two blocks.

Employees returning from parental leave may work a reduced 80% schedule at full pay for their
first 4 weeks back.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family
member, and up to 2 paid days for other relatives.

## Public Holidays

Lumora observes 11 public holidays per year in the United States. The holiday calendar for
each country is published in the People Portal every November for the following year.
