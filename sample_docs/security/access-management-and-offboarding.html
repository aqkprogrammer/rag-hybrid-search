Access Management & Offboarding Standard

This standard defines how access to Lumora systems is granted, reviewed and removed. It implements the least-privilege principle from the Information Security Policy.

Requesting Access

Access to internal systems is requested through the IT Service Portal. Each request must be approved by the employee's manager and by the system owner. Access to production systems additionally requires completion of the production access training.

Production access is granted just-in-time through the Gatekeeper tool for a maximum of 8 hours per session. Standing administrative access to production is not permitted.

Access Reviews

System owners review user access every quarter. Access that is no longer needed must be revoked within 5 business days of the review. Privileged access to production and finance systems is reviewed every month.

Offboarding

Voluntary departures

For voluntary departures, the manager must submit the offboarding ticket at least 5 business days before the employee's last day. All accounts are disabled at 17:00 local time on the last working day.

Involuntary departures

For involuntary terminations, access must be revoked within 1 hour of the termination meeting. People Operations notifies IT before the meeting takes place.

Returning equipment

  • Laptops and peripherals must be returned within 10 business days of the last day.
  • IT sends a prepaid shipping box to remote employees.
  • Returned laptops are securely wiped and reimaged before reuse.
